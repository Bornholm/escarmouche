```

texture. The character is the clear focal point, sharply rendered against an
atmospheric environment background with soft depth of field. Landscape
orientation, wide 16:9 framing with the subject slightly off-center. Palette
dominated by rust orange and bleached bone white tones over neutral shadows — strong value contrast so
the artwork stays readable when printed in greyscale on a 63×88 mm card.

Subject: A wiry sharpshooter woman lying atop a rusted car roof, long scoped rifle assembled from salvaged parts, goggles up on her forehead, ammo bandolier nearly empty, heat haze rising off the wreck.
```
